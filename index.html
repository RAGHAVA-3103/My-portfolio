University of Nebraska–Lincoln · Team research

Alternative cover crop termination

Our team is developing a ground robot that combines roller crimping with a second physical treatment to terminate cover crops without herbicides or tillage. I help run bench tests and field trials and document the results.

  • Field trials
  • Roller crimping

Personal & academic project

Autonomous UAV flight

I use software-in-the-loop simulation to develop takeoff, waypoint navigation, and return-to-launch workflows. I’m also experimenting with ROS 2 control loops and Raspberry Pi telemetry and logging.

  • PX4
  • ArduPilot
  • ROS 2
  • SITL

Ground robotics prototype

Manure-handling robot

I’m prototyping a ground vehicle for manure collection and transport. The work includes integrating GPS and inertial sensors, evaluating obstacle detection, and iterating on traction, payload handling, and power requirements.

  • Cube Orange
  • GPS / IMU
  • Motor control

Computer vision project

Weed locator

I’m preparing field imagery and evaluating baseline weed detection models. The goal is to turn detections into georeferenced maps and scouting files that can guide targeted treatment.

  • Field imagery
  • Precision & recall
  • Georeferencing
