Selected projects

Flight autonomy · In development

Autonomous UAV missions

I develop and simulate takeoff, waypoint navigation, and return-to-launch workflows. I’m building ROS 2 control loops that connect perception to flight commands and experimenting with Raspberry Pi for telemetry and logging.

  • PX4 / ArduPilot
  • ROS 2
  • SITL

UNL team research

Alternative cover crop termination

Our team is developing a compact robotic platform that combines roller crimping with a second physical treatment. I help test methods such as heat and UV-C and document the results.

University project overview ↗

Ground robotics · Prototype

Manure-handling robot

I’m prototyping an unmanned ground vehicle for manure collection and transport. The design uses a Cube Orange flight controller for IMU and GPS integration, with work on traction, motor control, payload handling, and obstacle sensing.

  • Cube Orange
  • IMU / GPS
  • Motor control

Computer vision · In development

Weed locator and field mapping

I’m preparing field imagery, training baseline weed-detection models, and evaluating precision and recall. The planned outputs are georeferenced maps and CSV files that can help target scouting and treatment.

  • UAV imagery
  • Computer vision
  • Georeferencing
